Experience
• Atomic Coffee (2023–2025) — Account Manager
Managed key accounts, ensuring optimal sales performance and brand visibility. Developed and executed promotional strategies driving increased distribution and revenue growth.
• Futurity (2021–2022) — Sales Manager
Led strategic sales planning, collaborating with marketing teams to optimise brand execution in key markets. Utilised CRM tools and data insights to enhance customer targeting.

Led the brand from launch to national distribution, building strong partnerships with retailers and distributors across 50+ stockists.
